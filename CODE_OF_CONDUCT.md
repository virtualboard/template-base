# Community Code of Conduct

VirtualBoard contributors are expected to collaborate professionally and make the
project welcoming to people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and project security boundaries.
- Make room for questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as unsafe or disruptive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate humiliation,
doxxing, credential exposure, and knowingly unsafe use of project automation are
not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository security advisory channel
when confidentiality is needed. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the project
and its community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and
free from conflicts of interest.
